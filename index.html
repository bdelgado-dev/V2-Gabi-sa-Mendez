A PHILIPPINE FOLKLORE SURVIVAL GAME

GABI SA MENDEZ

Survive five nights in a village haunted by creatures of Philippine folklore.

SETTINGS & CONTROLS

Single Player

A / D - Move left and right

W / S - Move up and down

T - Shoot

Y - Melee attack

U - Reload

I - Use healing / special item

O - Open / close backpack

B - Throw Bawang

1-5 - Select quick inventory slot

In the backpack: W / S choose, T equips

Esc - Pause

Two Player Co-op

Player 1

A / D / W / S - Move

T - Shoot

Y - Melee

U - Reload

I - Heal / special item

O - Backpack

B - Throw Bawang

Player 2

Arrow Keys - Move

Numpad 1 - Shoot

Numpad 2 - Melee

Numpad 4 - Reload

Numpad 5 - Heal / special item

Numpad 6 - Backpack

Numpad 7 - Throw Bawang

Numpad 8 / 9 - Switch quick slot

E - Hold to revive teammate

No numpad? Player 2 can use

. (period) - Shoot

/ - Melee

, (comma) - Reload

Right Shift - Heal / special item

Enter - Backpack

M - Throw Bawang

; and ' - Switch quick slot
