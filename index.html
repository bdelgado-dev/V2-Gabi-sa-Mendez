SETTINGS & CONTROLS

Single Player

A / D - Move left and right
W / S - Move up and down
T - Shoot
Y - Melee attack
U - Reload
I - Use healing / special item
O - Open / close backpack
B - Throw Bawang
1-5 - Select quick inventory slot
In the backpack: W / S choose, T equips. Esc pauses.

Two Player Co-op

Player 1A / D / W / S - Move
T - Shoot
Y - Melee
U - Reload
I - Heal / special item
O - Backpack
B - Throw BawangPlayer 2Arrow Keys - Move
Numpad 1 - Shoot
Numpad 2 - Melee
Numpad 4 - Reload
Numpad 5 - Heal / special item
Numpad 6 - Backpack
Numpad 7 - Throw Bawang
Numpad 8 / 9 - Switch quick slot
E - Hold to revive teammateNo numpad? Use these instead. (period) - Shoot
/ - Melee
, (comma) - Reload
Right Shift - Heal / special item
Enter - Backpack
M - Throw Bawang
; and ' - Switch quick slot
Player 2 also gets wider aim assist.
